GSC for Claude

A reporting integration for Google Search Console, Google Analytics, and Google Ads data.

GSC for Claude is an internal reporting tool used by Elite Information Tech to read website performance data on behalf of accounts that have granted it access - Search Console indexing/coverage data, Analytics reporting data, and Google Ads keyword search-volume data. It is used to generate SEO reports and audits for the accounts that connect it.

It does not create, modify, or delete any campaigns, properties, or account settings. It only reads data to produce reports.
